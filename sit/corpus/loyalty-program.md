Brightcart Plus members earn 2 points per 100 spent, and every point is worth 1 at checkout. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Membership costs 499 a year and includes free express delivery, early access to sales, and an extra 5 percent off on grocery orders over 1,500. Points cannot be earned on gift card purchases.
