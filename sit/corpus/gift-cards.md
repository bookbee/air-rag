Brightcart gift cards come in values from 500 to 10,000 and are valid for 12 months from purchase. They can be used online and in stores, across several orders, until the balance runs out. Gift cards cannot be returned, exchanged for cash, or used to buy other gift cards. A lost gift card can be blocked and reissued if you have its purchase receipt.
