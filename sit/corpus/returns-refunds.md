title: Returns and refunds
uri: https://help.brightcart.example/returns

---
Most items bought from Brightcart can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and come with the invoice or order number.
Start a return from My Orders > Return item, or at any Brightcart store. Once the returned item passes inspection, refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or as Brightcart store credit, whichever you choose.
These cannot be returned: perishable groceries, opened personal-care products, innerwear, and gift cards. Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat return pickup fee of 49 is deducted from the refund.
