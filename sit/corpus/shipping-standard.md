---
title: Standard delivery
uri: https://help.brightcart.example/shipping
kind: policy
---
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities and 5 to 8 business days elsewhere. It is free on orders of 499 or more; below that, a delivery fee of 40 applies. You get a tracking link by SMS and email once the order is dispatched. Deliveries are attempted twice before the parcel is returned to the warehouse.
