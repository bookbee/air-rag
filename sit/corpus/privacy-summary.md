Brightcart uses your name, address, phone number and order history to deliver orders, provide support, and personalise recommendations. We never sell personal data. Marketing messages need your consent and can be turned off any time in Settings > Notifications. You can download or delete your account data from Settings > Privacy; deletion is completed within 30 days.
