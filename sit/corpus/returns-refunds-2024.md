ARCHIVED: this policy was replaced on 1 April 2025. Most items bought from Brightcart could be returned within 15 days of delivery for a refund. The item had to be unused and in its original packaging.
Refunds were issued as store credit only, within 10 business days. Perishable groceries and gift cards could not be returned.
