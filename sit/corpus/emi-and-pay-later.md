No-cost EMI over 3 or 6 months is available on orders of 3,000 or more with selected credit cards. The interest is offered as an upfront discount, so you pay only the item price. Standard EMI over 9 to 24 months carries the bank's interest rate. Pay Later lets eligible customers pay within 15 days without interest.
