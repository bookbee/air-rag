---
title: Accepted payment methods
uri: https://help.brightcart.example/payments
kind: policy
---
Brightcart accepts UPI, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), net banking, Brightcart gift cards and wallet balance. Cash on delivery is available on orders up to 10,000. Saved cards are tokenised as RBI guidelines require; Brightcart never stores your full card number.
