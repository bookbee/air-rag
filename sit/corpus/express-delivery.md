uri: https://help.brightcart.example/express

Express delivery brings eligible items the next day in 40 cities, for a fee of 99. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12 noon in Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, for a fee of 149. Large appliances and furniture are not eligible for express or same-day delivery. Brightcart Plus members get express delivery free.
