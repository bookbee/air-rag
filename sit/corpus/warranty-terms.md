Every electronic item and appliance sold by Brightcart comes with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 12 months from the invoice date unless the product page says otherwise. Brightcart Assured products carry an extra 12 months of extended warranty, honoured by Brightcart directly.
What is covered: manufacturing defects in parts and workmanship that appear during normal household use. Covered repairs, parts and labour are free. If the same fault is repaired three times within the warranty period, the product is replaced.
What is not covered: physical damage, liquid damage, damage from voltage fluctuations without a stabiliser, consumables such as filters, blades, bulbs and batteries, cosmetic wear, and any product used commercially. Unauthorised repairs or tampering with the serial number void the warranty.
How to claim: keep the invoice, which you can download again from My Orders. Raise a claim from My Orders > Warranty claim, or call the manufacturer's service centre listed on the warranty card. For large appliances a technician visits within 72 hours in metro cities. Small appliances may need to be dropped at a service centre or picked up by courier.
Extended warranty: Brightcart Assured extended warranty starts the day the manufacturer's warranty ends. It covers the same faults, is transferable once if you gift the product, and cannot be cancelled after 30 days. Claims under the extended warranty are handled by Brightcart Care on 1800-000-0000, between 9am and 9pm every day.
Installation: a free installation is included for air conditioners, washing machines and water purifiers, and must be booked within 15 days of delivery. Wall-mounting brackets and extra piping are charged separately.
