uri: https://help.brightcart.example/price-match

If you find a lower price for an identical item on brightcart.example within 7 days of your purchase, we refund the difference as store credit. The price match does not cover lightning deals, clearance items, or competitors' websites. Request it from My Orders > Price drop refund.
