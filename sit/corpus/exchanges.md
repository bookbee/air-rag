title: Exchanges and size swaps
uri: https://help.brightcart.example/exchanges

Clothing and footwear can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery, once per order line. The replacement ships free of charge once the pickup is complete. If the new size is out of stock, the exchange becomes a regular return and a refund is issued. Electronics and appliances cannot be exchanged; return them instead.
