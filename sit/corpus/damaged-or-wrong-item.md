uri: https://help.brightcart.example/damaged

If an item arrives damaged, is defective, or is not what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report a problem, with photos of the item and its packaging. We arrange a free pickup and send a replacement, or refund you if a replacement isn't available. For a damaged grocery item, the refund is instant once the photos are reviewed, and you don't need to send it back.
