---
title: Cancelling an order
uri: https://help.brightcart.example/cancel
kind: policy
---
You can cancel an order without charge until it is dispatched, from My Orders > Cancel. A prepaid order is refunded automatically within 3 business days of cancellation. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse delivery at the door, and it is treated as a return. Grocery slot orders can be cancelled until 2 hours before the delivery slot starts.
